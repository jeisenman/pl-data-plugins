# RA workflow report 06: loaded relationships and core accounts in the Pricing UI

## Why this stage matters

This is the point at which an RA load becomes a banker decision: the selected relationship determines the customer context, existing account inventory, relationship-level assumptions, and the baseline financial statements against which an opportunity is priced. Correctness is more than showing the right relationship name. Pricing must combine the loaded relationship with the *current opportunity and scenario state* so that payoffs, at-risk accounts, overrides, assumptions, and treasury changes produce a defensible profitability result.

There are two deliberately different paths:

| Path | Purpose | Pricing consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Display / selection** | Find a relationship; show its hierarchy, details, and the existing core accounts. | Gives a banker the data needed to select the correct customer and inspect current exposure. It is not itself the final calculation. |
| **Calculated impact** | Recalculate the loaded RA relationship in the saved opportunity/scenario context. | Supplies the current/if-we-win/if-we-lose/payoff-or-renewal impact used by the Pricing UI; it includes opportunity-specific changes rather than just the raw RA baseline. |

This distinction prevents a common diagnostic error: a relationship can display successfully while calculated impact is absent or limited because the opportunity is new, has not been saved, or its persisted relationship differs from the currently selected one ([OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5588](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5588)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5602](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5602)).

4. **Pricing loads display context and begins impact calculation.** `useRelationshipSlimStore` concurrently requests `RelationshipSlimForOpportunity`, client override *settings*, and the details summary; it then loads the relationship assumption set and starts calculated-impact loading without blocking the display ([relationshipSlimStore.ts:132](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipSlimStore.ts#L132)-[relationshipSlimStore.ts:188](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipSlimStore.ts#L188)). 5. **Account grids load on demand.** For example, the loan grid asks for the relationship's slim loans scoped to the opportunity ([relationshipService.ts:61](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/relationshipService.ts#L61)-[relationshipService.ts:69](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/relationshipService.ts#L69)); a clicked loan then loads detail by relationship and account IDs ([coreAccountStore.ts:34](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/coreAccountStore.ts#L34)-[coreAccountStore.ts:68](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/coreAccountStore.ts#L68)). ## API, authorization, and service path

The frontend service is a thin typed HTTP boundary. `RelationshipController` is routed beneath `application/Relationship`, requires a client user, and marks the response as confidential ([RelationshipController.cs:12](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L12)-[RelationshipController.cs:20](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L20)-[RelationshipController.cs:20)). It parses both IDs before delegating the selected relationship, summary, and account endpoints ([RelationshipController.cs:55](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L55)-[RelationshipController.cs:123](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L123)-[RelationshipController.cs:123)).

`DomainClientUserAccessProxy` is the significant authorization boundary: for opportunity-scoped relationship reads it calls `ThrowExceptionIfCurrentUserCannotSeeOpportunityOrRelationshipNew`, then passes session-scoped `RelationshipNewId` and `OpportunityId` values to the business services ([DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2201](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/Access/DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs#L2201)-[DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2213](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/Access/DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs#L2213)). The same check protects baseline and calculated relationship impact ([DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2227](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/Access/DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs#L2227)-[DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2292](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/Access/DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs#L2292)). A successful UI request therefore does not imply unrestricted relationship access.

The relationship business service gives display APIs a subtle but important override rule. For `RelationshipSlimForOpportunity`, if the opportunity has RA overrides it builds from a full relationship family summary with overrides applied; otherwise it reads the slim object directly from the selected persistence path ([RelationshipBusinessService.cs:501](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipBusinessService.cs#L501)-[RelationshipBusinessService.cs:558](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipBusinessService.cs#L558)-[RelationshipBusinessService.cs:558)). The account-grid service follows the same rule: with overrides it creates a hierarchy-wide list from the overridden family; without them it obtains the slim grid from relationship persistence ([RelationshipBusinessService.cs:692](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipBusinessService.cs#L692)-[RelationshipBusinessService.cs:754](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipBusinessService.cs#L754)-[RelationshipBusinessService.cs:754)). This is why correcting an override can change what a banker sees *and* what the price calculation starts from.

For relationship-service-enabled clients, `RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper` switches calls to the external Relationship Service API; otherwise it uses the configured local persistence implementation ([RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs:24](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs#L24)-[RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs:33](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs#L33)-[RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs:33), [RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs:88](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs#L88)-[RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs:104](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs#L104)-[RelationshipServicePersistenceHelper.cs:104)). The feature flag is explicitly named `Use Relationship Service` in the client feature-flag accessor ([featureFlagSettings.ts:290](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/access/featureFlagSettings.ts#L290)-[featureFlagSettings.ts:292](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/access/featureFlagSettings.ts#L292)-[featureFlagSettings.ts:292)). The API persistence synchronously waits for the external summary/details calls and logs then rethrows exceptions ([RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs:73](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Persistence/External/RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs#L73)-[RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs:130](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Persistence/External/RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs#L130)-[RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs:130)).

## Calculation flow: why RA affects price rather than merely context

The calculated-impact endpoint delegates to `OpportunityBusinessService`, not just `RelationshipBusinessService` ([DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2282](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/Access/DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs#L2282)-[DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2292](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/Access/DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs#L2292)-[DomainClientUserAccessProxy.cs:2292)). It:

1. requires the RA baseline's current FTP and Strategic financial statements;
2. retrieves the persisted opportunity and detects a changed selected relationship;
3. resolves the override-aware relationship for that opportunity;
4. derives fully paid-off core account references, active payoff and at-risk values, and relationship assumptions;
5. refreshes relationship-derived opportunity snapshots and recalculates the opportunity before producing scenario impacts.

Those mechanics are present in [OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5571](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5571)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5665](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5665)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5665). In plain terms, RA supplies the starting balance sheet and historical profitability; Pricing applies the banker’s proposed deal and scenario to determine incremental and relationship-level profitability. A bad RA account relationship, stale baseline, or incorrectly applied override can therefore alter payoff selection, at-risk treatment, balance/volume context, and the displayed profitability decision.

## Data contracts and feature/package gates

The display contract is intentionally split:

- `RelationshipSlim` is the lightweight identity/detail/header contract, including hierarchy references and `CoreAccountSummarySlim` counts ([relationshipSlim.ts:8](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/models/relationshipSlim.ts#L8)-[relationshipSlim.ts:27](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/models/relationshipSlim.ts#L27)-[relationshipSlim.ts:27)).
- `RelationshipSummary` is the materialized hierarchy contract with loans, deposits, others, treasury accounts/deposits, billable groups, associated opportunities, and parent references ([relationshipSummary.ts:12](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/models/relationshipSummary.ts#L12)-[relationshipSummary.ts:35](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/models/relationshipSummary.ts#L35)-[relationshipSummary.ts:35)).

Package settings control whether a client has relationships/core integration, hierarchy, selector, and relationship overrides; `alwaysShowOpportunityRelationshipDetails` controls display behavior without a selected relationship ([relationshipAwarenessPackageSettings.ts:3](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/models/relationshipAwarenessPackageSettings.ts#L3)-[relationshipAwarenessPackageSettings.ts:15](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/models/relationshipAwarenessPackageSettings.ts#L15)-[relationshipAwarenessPackageSettings.ts:15)). ## Overrides, caching, and consistency

Core-account and opportunity relationship overrides are mutable pricing inputs. The UI retrieves client override settings and opportunity-scoped override summaries; saving core-account overrides POSTs both relationship-level and opportunity-level values, then clears relationship-service caches ([coreOverrideService.ts:13](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/coreOverrideService.ts#L13)-[coreOverrideService.ts:76](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/coreOverrideService.ts#L76)-[coreOverrideService.ts:76)). The controller rejects an empty save request and invokes the appropriate business-service save methods ([RelationshipController.cs:675](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L675)-[RelationshipController.cs:700](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L700)-[RelationshipController.cs:700)).

The common frontend cache is an in-memory `Map` keyed only by JSON-serialized arguments, contains promises (including a rejected promise), and has no TTL or automatic invalidation ([cacheService.ts:9](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/cacheService.ts#L9)-[cacheService.ts:30](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/cacheService.ts#L30)). `RelationshipService.clearAllCache` explicitly clears its cached relationship/impact endpoint functions ([relationshipService.ts:284](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/relationshipService.ts#L284)-[relationshipService.ts:299](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Common/services/relationshipService.ts#L299)); override saving calls that clearer. The relationship impact store additionally versions in-flight loads, preventing a late, stale response from replacing the most recent relationship calculation ([relationshipImpactStore.ts:75](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipImpactStore.ts#L75)-[relationshipImpactStore.ts:80](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipImpactStore.ts#L80), [relationshipImpactStore.ts:282](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipImpactStore.ts#L282)-[relationshipImpactStore.ts:287](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipImpactStore.ts#L287)).

**Inference:** a successful external RA reload is not sufficient to refresh an already-open Pricing page; the shown frontend cache is cleared on override save but no general RA-import-driven invalidation is evidenced in this stage. Verify the wider application event/refresh path before promising bankers immediate post-upload visibility.

## Failure modes and banker-visible behavior

- **Missing/invalid identifiers:** relationship-controller endpoints return 404 or 400 for malformed IDs, before reaching data access ([RelationshipController.cs:55](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L55)-[RelationshipController.cs:67](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L67)-[RelationshipController.cs:67), [RelationshipController.cs:923](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L923)-[RelationshipController.cs:936](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Web/APIControllers/Application/RelationshipController.cs#L936)-[RelationshipController.cs:936)).
- **No authorization or a deleted/nonexistent relationship:** access-proxy checks or business services throw; UI slim-store handling reports an error and clears relationship/override state, then marks loading complete ([relationshipSlimStore.ts:201](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipSlimStore.ts#L201)-[relationshipSlimStore.ts:208](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipSlimStore.ts#L208)-[relationshipSlimStore.ts:208)).
- **New/unsaved opportunity or relationship changed after save:** server returns base impact without opportunity/scenario data; client-side calculation must complete the picture ([OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5594](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5594)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5602](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5602)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5602)).
- **Invalid baseline financial statements:** a missing FTP or Strategic current statement is a bad request, protecting Pricing from presenting a partial number as a valid RA calculation ([OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5579](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5579)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5590](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/OpportunityBusinessService.cs#L5590)-[OpportunityBusinessService.cs:5590)).
- **Relationship Service outage or contract failure:** external persistence logs the method, relationship ID, and exception, then rethrows rather than silently falling back to database data ([RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs:84](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Persistence/External/RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs#L84)-[RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs:106](../../repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Persistence/External/RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs#L106)-[RelationshipServiceApiPersistence.cs:106)).
- **Concurrent selection/calculation:** selection shows a loading state around bridge/store synchronization, and impact load versioning prevents an older calculation response from winning ([RelationshipSelectorDialog.vue:331](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/opportunity/components/RelationshipSelectorDialog.vue#L331)-[RelationshipSelectorDialog.vue:350](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/opportunity/components/RelationshipSelectorDialog.vue#L350)-[RelationshipSelectorDialog.vue:350), [relationshipImpactStore.ts:282](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipImpactStore.ts#L282)-[relationshipImpactStore.ts:287](../../repositories/pl-application/ClientApp/Pricing/src/modules/relationship/stores/relationshipImpactStore.ts#L287)-[relationshipImpactStore.ts:287)).

## Boundaries and open questions

- This report traces consumption after RA data is loaded; it does **not** establish the exact upstream transformer-to-Relationship-Service publication mechanism. That belongs to the loaded/persistence stage.
- **Unknown from the inspected code:** the precise server-side cache invalidation/event propagation after an RA upload completes. The frontend cache behavior is evidenced above, but a full end-to-end post-import freshness guarantee needs the importer and hosting/event path.
- **Inference:** the UI's `Use Relationship Service` flag and the backend helper flag are intended to select the same data authority, but verify their feature-flag rollout configuration per client rather than assuming they are always aligned.
