# RA workflow report 03: import mapping, duplicate resolution, and validation

## Why this stage matters

This is the RA workflow's **contract-enforcement and evidence-preservation boundary**. It turns client-specific uploaded rows into typed relationship, account, association, collateral, fee, and participation records while retaining source headers, values, and row number. It also decides which duplicate records are safe enough to retain in the *validated* view. That is important because an apparently small mapping/configuration change can change relationship ownership, account membership, extract date, and the modified flags that tell downstream processing whether the uploaded extract differs from the prior one.

The stage deliberately has two products:

1. **Raw mapping results** preserve the mapped (including failed) results plus source context for operations and the later V2 transform.
2. **Validated mapping results** are a filtered/de-duplicated indexed view for inspection and diagnostics.

The distinction is operationally important: V2 is explicitly queued with the *unvalidated* mapping-results blob, because that transform performs its own validation. Mapping validation therefore improves visibility and produces a cleaner comparison view; it is not, by itself, the gate that changes the V2 transform input. See [RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:237](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:237) through [RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:254](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:254).

## Duplicate policy: why it protects relationship integrity

Validation groups successful records by their external identifiers for relationships, loans, deposits, others, and participations ([MappingValidation.cs:24](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/MappingValidation.cs:24)-[MappingValidation.cs:45](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/MappingValidation.cs:24)-[MappingValidation.cs:45)). Duplicate accounts that span multiple relationship identifiers are recorded as errors before filtering, which protects against accidentally assigning the same account to different relationships. Participation duplicates across loans are also errors rather than automatically resolved.

For same-relationship duplicates, both policies permit a relationship-name-only difference and prefer a non-wildcard owner when that is the sole relevant difference. Conservative loan handling only auto-selects the non-`SinglePay` record for constrained payment-type/frequency differences; otherwise it rejects the ambiguity ([ConservativeDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:15](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/DuplicateAccountHandling/ConservativeDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:15)-[ConservativeDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:76](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/DuplicateAccountHandling/ConservativeDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:15)-[ConservativeDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:76)). Aggressive handling still refuses a loan whose relationship identifier differs, but otherwise selects by payment (zero loses; then lower payment wins); it does not resolve duplicate deposits or other accounts ([AggressiveDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:14](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/DuplicateAccountHandling/AggressiveDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:14)-[AggressiveDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:76](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/DuplicateAccountHandling/AggressiveDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:14)-[AggressiveDuplicateImportItemResolver.cs:76)).

The resolver compares public readable properties except `RowValues`; any non-comparable new property becomes a failure reason. This makes contract evolution visible rather than silently merging an unfamiliar value ([DuplicateImportItemResolverBase.cs:12](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/DuplicateAccountHandling/DuplicateImportItemResolverBase.cs:12)-[DuplicateImportItemResolverBase.cs:109](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.DataImport.Mapping/Validation/DuplicateAccountHandling/DuplicateImportItemResolverBase.cs:12)-[DuplicateImportItemResolverBase.cs:109)).

- **Re-entry/racing run:** both service methods reject an already-started non-ready phase via `DataIngressMultiRunException`, which the process manager persists without publishing a competing stopped event ([RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:499](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:499)-[RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:519](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:499)-[RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:519), [RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:629](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:629)-[RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:649](/Users/jeisenman/projects/pl-data-plugins/repositories/pl-application/PrecisionLender.Application.Core/Business/Direct/RA/RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:629)-[RelationshipAwarenessBusinessService.cs:649)).
